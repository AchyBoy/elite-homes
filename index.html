How It Goes

Five steps, and you are never guessing where you stand.

01

Sit Down

An hour at our table. Your lot, your list, and an honest number before anybody draws anything.

02

Design

We draw it with you, not at you. Changes here are free. Changes after framing are not.

03

Selections

Every finish picked and priced before the slab is poured, so the number you sign is the number you pay.

04
